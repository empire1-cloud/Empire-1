# Lyrica 3 Founding Creator Pilot

## Campaign thesis

AI music generation is not the full problem. Independent creators also need to know who owns the output, how identity and lineage are preserved, what changed during remixing, and how future payouts are traced.

The pilot recruits creators who already make music but distrust AI because ownership, attribution, and payout visibility disappear inside the tool.

## Sharp customer

An independent artist, producer, beatmaker, or songwriter who:

- controls at least one original song or beat
- wants creative speed without surrendering ownership clarity
- has experienced unclear rights, attribution, lineage, or payout handling
- is willing to test one real workflow and give direct feedback

## Offer

**Bring one song. Keep the proof.**

A small founding-creator pilot testing one creator-owned song or beat through:

1. creation or intake
2. DNA identity
3. Soulprint
4. VICS signing
5. ledger evidence
6. payout evidence when the live payout path supports it

## Intake

The creator intake form collects:

- creator identity and contact information
- creator role and genre
- the problem they have experienced with current music or AI tools
- confirmation that they control a testable song or beat
- the proof outcome that matters most
- willingness to test, review evidence, and provide feedback
- explicit confirmation that submitted music is owned or authorized

## Outreach sequence

### Day 0 — Direct invitation

Lead with the proof-first pilot rather than an AI product pitch.

### Day 2 — Ownership problem

Explain that most tools stop at generation and the pilot tests what happens after the file exists.

### Day 5 — Proof story

Publish the six-layer workflow only with dated evidence and clear status labels.

### Day 8 — Small-cohort close

Explain that the first group stays small so each creator receives a real proof review.

## Success targets

- 20 qualified creator replies
- 10 completed applications
- 5 pilot starts
- 3 completed proof chains
- 2 paid conversions

These are targets, not current traction claims.

## Weekly readout

Track:

- messages sent
- qualified replies
- applications
- pilot starts
- proof chains completed
- paid conversions
- top objection
- broken workflow step
- time from outreach to pilot start
- time from pilot start to completed proof chain

## Integrity rules

- Never publish target numbers as achieved results.
- Require creators to confirm ownership or permission before accepting music.
- Do not publish payout claims until the live payout path produces evidence.
- Keep the 70/30 creator split and $1.25 remix payout tied to governing terms and verified implementation.
- Separate verified facts, founder interpretation, targets, and pending proof.
- Record creator feedback before changing positioning or product claims.
- Keep Lyrica 3 distinct from the broader Empire-1 umbrella and other product universes.

## Operating decision

The GTM stack is now in proof mode. This campaign exists to determine whether the new GTM surfaces reduce campaign creation time and produce qualified creator participation, completed proof chains, and paid continuation.
