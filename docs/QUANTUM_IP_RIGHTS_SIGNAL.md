Empire-1 should prepare for cryptographic migration without claiming current quantum safety:

- version signing algorithms and key types
- keep signatures separate from the underlying business record

- support dual signing during migration
- preserve old verification evidence
- allow records to be re-signed without rewriting their history
- maintain revocation and migration receipts
