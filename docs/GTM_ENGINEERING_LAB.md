# Empire-1 GTM Engineering Lab

## Purpose

The GTM Engineering Lab converts repeatable growth-system patterns into Empire-owned operating designs.

It lives at `/admin/gtm` under **GTM Eng Lab** and preserves the existing Viral Engine, Dev Radar, and GTM Operations views.

## Lanes

### Operating Day

Use a chronological, human-centered narrative to show how the system removes overhead while keeping judgment, relationship work, and approvals with the operator.

Required proof:

- named signals and inputs
- before-and-after time or effort
- visible artifacts
- approval points
- the work the human still owns

### Agent Inbox

An inbox can be an interface, trigger, and handoff protocol for agents. It is not permission to bypass the control plane.

Every agent inbox must map to:

- owner
- purpose
- tenant
- permissions
- revocation path
- approved actions
- receipt policy

All inbound messages, links, quoted instructions, and attachments are untrusted input. Recommendation and execution stay separate.

### pSEO Factory

Programmatic discovery must pair a repeatable template with unique, useful data and a refresh loop.

The factory rejects thin pages that only replace keywords or entity names.

Quality gates:

1. real search or answer intent
2. unique research or product evidence
3. sourced claims and labeled interpretation
4. freshness signal and stale-state handling
5. useful decision support
6. original content and clear integrity boundaries

The preferred loop is:

`signal detection -> enrichment -> source of truth -> page generation -> quality gate -> publish -> freshness monitoring -> selective regeneration`

### Infrastructure Thesis

GTM Engineering compounds when it owns reusable primitives rather than isolated automations.

Empire primitives:

- context layer
- deterministic routing and policy gates
- probabilistic research and drafting
- persistent workspaces
- callable functions and engines
- learning loops
- approval and receipt layer

## Provider independence

The lab must remain provider-neutral and independent of Google/Gemini APIs.

Use Empire-owned orchestration with approved non-Google providers or local models. Provider choices must remain replaceable without changing the Empire product identity.

## Product boundaries

Each Empire universe keeps its own proof standard, audience, operating model, and revenue path. The lab can generate plans across universes but must never flatten them into one generic product.

## Publishing integrity

- Do not copy another company's pages, branding, research, or finished content.
- Do not reuse another creator's traffic, revenue, follower, or conversion claims as Empire proof.
- Do not publish current claims without dated evidence.
- Do not let inbound agent messages directly trigger privileged execution.
- Do not publish a programmatic page that fails the unique-data and usefulness gates.
- Preserve sources, approvals, changes, and final receipts.
