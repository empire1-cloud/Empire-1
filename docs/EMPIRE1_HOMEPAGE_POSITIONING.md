# Empire1.cloud Homepage Positioning

## Core Principle

**Empire Revenue OS is the front door.**
**Hybrid Intelligence Core is the engine underneath.**

The homepage should make visitors understand in 10 seconds:
> "Empire Revenue OS turns my business into a revenue system. I can try it now."

## Before the Refocus

The homepage was confusing because it felt like two products fighting each other:

1. **Public Revenue OS** — tryable product with hero, proof bar, stats chips
2. **Enterprise HIC / Emergent DNA architecture consulting** — DNA Score, Five Core Principles, $5K–$250K tiers, $78K MRR claims, engine vault, anime engines, arcade, multiverse content

The HIC architecture content appeared immediately after the hero, before visitors understood what the product did or that they could try it.

## After the Refocus

### New Section Order

1. **Revenue OS Hero** — "Turn your business into a revenue system by Monday"
2. **What Revenue OS Generates** — 6 capability cards
3. **Start Here Pricing** — Free Demo ($0) · Revenue Receipt ($299) · Revenue Sprint ($999)
4. **How It Works** — 5 steps
5. **Who It Is For** — 6 audience cards + anti-ICP note
6. **GTM Layer** — ICP scoring, buyer tiers, signals, scoring model
7. **Powered by Hybrid Intelligence Core** — 8 engine capabilities
8. **Implementation & Enterprise Pricing** — $5K+ / $50K+ / 25% rev share
9. **Empire Ecosystem** — footer-level links (SLA113, Lyrica, Cultura, Southern, Soulfire, Archisynapse, HIC)
10. **Final CTA** — Try demo · Generate Receipt · Talk to Architect

### Content Safety

No unverified claims are presented as real proof. The homepage shows:

- Public try flow exists
- Backend run returns Revenue OS output
- GTM layer scores buyers
- Demo recording available
- Delivery receipt generated
- Manual payment fallback works

No fake traction. No unverified MRR, ARR, or operator counts.
