SLA113 OPERATOR QUICKCARD
=========================

This document provides a high-level overview of the SLA113 engine layer.
All engines are integrated Python services importable by SLA113 routers.

------------------------------------------------------------
ENGINE: SLA113VolatilityEngine
FILE: sla113_volatility_engine.py
METHOD: compute(payload)
PURPOSE:
    Computes volatility values for operator-level diagnostics.
------------------------------------------------------------

ENGINE: SLA113TerminationEngine
FILE: sla113_termination_engine.py
METHOD: evaluate(payload)
PURPOSE:
    Evaluates termination thresholds and impact events.
------------------------------------------------------------

ENGINE: SLA113FaultEngine
FILE: sla113_fault_engine.py
METHOD: analyze(payload)
PURPOSE:
    Detects, classifies, and reports operator-level faults.
------------------------------------------------------------

ENGINE: SLA113SettlementEngine
FILE: sla113_settlement_engine.py
METHOD: settle(payload)
PURPOSE:
    Performs settlement and reconciliation calculations.
------------------------------------------------------------

NOTES:
- All engines are located in /root/backend/app/engines/sla113/
- All engines follow SLA113 naming conventions.
- All engines are importable by SLA113 routers and services.
- This QuickCard is for operator reference only.
