Status: release candidate

Intake route: `/services/intake`

## Purpose

Applied AI Services is the cash-generating front door for Empire-1. It converts existing operating capabilities into scoped client engagements while preserving every product universe as an independent business.

This lane must:

1. Generate service revenue now.
2. Produce real customer evidence.
3. Identify repeated workflows that may become products later.
4. Fund product development without flattening Empire-1, SLA113, Lyrica 3, Cultura, Southern, Archisynapse, HIC, or the private Cofounder.
5. Avoid selling capability that has not been proven or staffed.

## Offer ladder

### 1. AI Operations Audit

Published range: **$750–$1,500**  
Target delivery: **5–7 business days**

Deliverables:

- Current-state workflow map
- Tool and integration inventory
- Revenue leakage and follow-up findings
- AI/data risk inventory
- Ranked opportunity matrix
- 30-day implementation roadmap
- Recommended next engagement

Acceptance gate:

- One named business outcome
- One accountable client owner
- Evidence supporting the top three findings
- Clear build/no-build recommendation

### 2. AI Automation Sprint

Published range: **$2,500–$7,500**  
Target delivery: **2–4 weeks**

Deliverables:

- One complete workflow from trigger to outcome
- Human approval and exception paths
- Activity/evidence logging
- Test scenarios and results
- Handoff guide
- Launch checklist

Acceptance gate:

- Happy path passes
- Failure path is visible
- Human override works
- No hidden execution behavior
- Client can operate the delivered workflow

### 3. AI Governance Readiness

Published range: **$3,500–$12,000**  
Target delivery: **3–6 weeks**

Deliverables may include:

- AI system inventory
- Vendor and model inventory
- Data-flow map
- AI risk register
- Human oversight matrix
- Access-control review
- Incident-readiness procedure
- Evidence register
- Policy templates
- Readiness gap summary

Boundary:

Empire-1 provides implementation and readiness support. Empire-1 does not issue SOC reports, legal opinions, regulatory certifications, or independent audit attestations.

### 4. Managed Improvement

Published range: **$1,000–$3,000 per month**

Deliverables:

- Monthly operating review
- Workflow performance and failure analysis
- Approved improvements
- Updated evidence and documentation
- Next-month priorities

Boundary:

This is not 24/7 managed detection and response, emergency incident response, or an unlimited development retainer.

## Qualification standard

A lead is qualified when all five are known:

1. **Problem:** What work is broken, slow, expensive, or uncontrolled?
2. **Outcome:** What measurable result should change?
3. **Owner:** Who approves decisions and accepts delivery?
4. **Systems:** Which tools, data, and integrations are involved?
5. **Boundary:** What cannot be exposed, automated, changed, or promised?

Budget and timeline should be known before proposing a sprint.

## Default sales path

1. Lead completes `/services/intake` or emails the founder.
2. Founder classifies the request: audit, sprint, governance, managed improvement, enterprise, licensing, or no-fit.
3. If scope is unclear, sell the Audit first.
4. If one workflow and outcome are clear, propose a Sprint.
5. If the request is primarily evidence, oversight, vendor/model control, or policy, propose Governance Readiness.
6. If the client needs infrastructure licensing or private deployment, route to Enterprise.
7. Never expose or sell the private Empire Auto Cofounder.

## Scope guardrails

Do not promise:

- Guaranteed revenue or cost savings
- SOC 2 certification or a SOC report
- Legal or regulatory compliance conclusions
- 24/7 security monitoring
- Autonomous high-impact decisions without human review
- Production integrations before credential, data, and access requirements are known
- Unlimited revisions or undefined ongoing work

## Discovery call structure

### Opening

“Tell me where work currently gets stuck, what that costs you, and what a successful fix would look like.”

### Required questions

- What triggers the workflow?
- Who touches it today?
- Where does it fail or slow down?
- What data enters and leaves?
- Which decision must remain human?
- What evidence must exist afterward?
- What is the value of fixing it this quarter?

### Close

Recommend one of three actions:

- Audit first
- Scoped sprint
- Not a fit / qualified partner required

Do not end a call with an undefined “custom AI solution.”

## Proposal skeleton

### Business problem

State the current workflow and its measurable cost or risk.

### Target outcome

State the result the engagement must produce.

### Scope

List included triggers, systems, approvals, outputs, and evidence.

### Exclusions

List all out-of-scope systems, certifications, security operations, legal conclusions, and future phases.

### Delivery plan

- Diagnose
- Design
- Build
- Prove
- Handoff

### Commercial terms

Use a fixed project fee for defined work. Define the deposit, milestone, final payment, client dependencies, and change-control process before work begins.

## Evidence package for every engagement

Each completed engagement should leave:

- Signed scope
- Current-state map
- Target-state map
- Decision and approval record
- Test evidence
- Known limitations
- Delivery receipt
- Client acceptance or open-items record
- An anonymized case-study candidate, subject to client approval
